What is the Bonus Race World Finals?

The week the best barrel racers and breakaway ropers come to Shawnee to run for the Bonus.

Every year the Bonus Race World Finals takes over the Heart of Oklahoma Expo Center in Shawnee, Oklahoma, for a full week of fast runs, big paychecks and serious bragging rights, produced by Renea Bolling and Double B Productions. Win a qualifier at an approved event, earn your spot, and come run for the title. The Finals feature the Bonus Race, the $10,000 Added Open Jackpot and the Ultimate Bonus Hi-Stakes Race, plus breakaway roping with its own Open 3D and sidepots. Youth, adult and senior sidepots mean there’s a chance to win for every rider. The next Finals are June 8-13, 2027. Be there!

To collect payment of winnings

We cannot mail winnings until we have a signed W-9 form (for the current year) on file with your address and social security number, signed by you. We will no longer accept a picture (jpg) of the form: it must be a completed PDF if you are emailing it, otherwise mail a hard copy to PO Box 306, Checotah, OK 74426. W-9 forms are always available at the show office.
