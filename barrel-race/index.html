General info

Added money for the Bonus Race World Finals is compiled from $1/run from approved qualifier events held in the previous year. The qualifying year for the 2027 Finals is January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.

Each qualifier event or qualifier series awards “qualifiers” to enter the Bonus Race World Finals. Once you’ve earned a qualifier, you are eligible to enter the BRWF the following year. Multiple qualifiers can be earned. A rider can enter unlimited times, but a horse can only run in the BRWF 2 times maximum.

Added money for the Open Jackpot is $10,000 added and is sponsored by Double B Productions, LLC. You do not have to earn a qualifier to enter the Open Jackpot — it is open to everyone.

The Bonus Race (added money based on $1/run from the previous year’s approved events) gives full awards through 10th place plus buckles to division leaders from every session. A horse may run 2x max in this race (as long as they choose different “sessions” to enter). A unique qualifier ID# is required for each entry made in this race. Under no circumstances may a horse run more than 2x. Riders who “win” qualifiers on site in the Open Jackpot may add on to any session of the Bonus Race as a late entry.

To collect winnings, return a completed W-9 form in the name of the rider, including social security number, address, signature and date, by email (scanned copies only, no jpgs) to bbbarrelbash@yahoo.com or by mail to Double B Productions, PO Box 306, Checotah, OK 74426.
